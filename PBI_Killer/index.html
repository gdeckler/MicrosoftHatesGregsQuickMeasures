Data coverage & methodology

Source: World Championship.xlsx. All numeric observations are embedded in this page; the report works offline and does not fetch external data. Blank and nonnumeric cells are treated as missing, never as zero. No interpolation or population weighting is used.

European country reference uses the 51 entries in the Countries worksheet, joined by country name. Moldova, Netherlands, Russia, and United Kingdom use normalized names. The all-workbook scope includes every country entry in the measure sheets; entries absent from the reference have no sub-region. Population entries may include territories or source aggregates. This report does not create a composite championship score.

Coverage: population 2000–2023; renewable energy 2004–2023; cultural participation 2006, 2015, 2022; life satisfaction 2013, 2018, 2021–2024. Year options cover recorded observations; the Years reference worksheet extends to 2025 but contains no additional measure observations. Percent measures retain the workbook units; life satisfaction retains its source score without assuming a scale.

Latest available chooses the most recent numeric value at or before the selected year, which may be older than that year. Two measures can consequently use different observation years. Exact year avoids that mismatch. Summaries show an unweighted mean across countries with available values, except population, which shows a total of available entries.
